Why AI Ethics Matters

June 19, 2026

Artificial intelligence and data analytics are becoming increasingly important in helping companies understand customer behavior and make better decisions. Businesses use data to identify trends, improve products, create recommendations, and develop marketing strategies. However, the use of consumer data also creates important ethical responsibilities related to privacy, fairness, transparency, and accountability.

When most people think about smartphone data, they probably imagine charts showing the most popular brands or the average price of a new device. While those insights are useful, they also reveal something much bigger: how businesses use data to understand consumers and make decisions. This is where AI, data analytics, and ethics all come together.

As part of my learning journey in data analytics, I created a Power BI dashboard to explore smartphone purchasing behavior. The dashboard analyzes information such as smartphone brands, prices, operating systems, RAM, battery capacity, and release dates. The goal was to practice transforming raw data into meaningful visualizations that help answer business questions.

Smartphone Purchasing Behavior Analysis Using Power BI#

The dashboard includes several key insights:

  • The most popular smartphone brands in the dataset.
  • The average price of smartphones by brand.
  • Price trends over different release years.
  • The market share of Android and iOS devices.
  • The average RAM offered by each smartphone brand.
  • Summary metrics, including the total number of smartphones analyzed, average price, and average battery capacity.

At first, this may look like a typical business analytics project. Companies can use this type of information to understand customer preferences, monitor market trends, and make better business decisions. However, once AI begins using this data to recommend products, predict buying behavior, or personalize advertisements, ethical questions become just as important as the analysis itself.

Privacy#

One of the biggest concerns when using AI and data analytics is privacy. Smartphone companies collect large amounts of information about customers, including purchasing habits, browsing activity, and device usage. While this data can help improve products and services, companies have a responsibility to protect personal information and clearly explain how it is being used.

Bias#

Bias is another important issue because AI systems learn from historical data. If that data only represents certain groups of customers or purchasing behaviors, the recommendations produced by AI may unintentionally favor one brand, price range, or type of consumer over another.

For example, an AI system trained mostly on premium smartphone purchases may consistently recommend expensive devices while overlooking budget-friendly options that might better meet a customer’s needs.

Transparency#

Transparency is important because users should understand how AI systems make decisions. One advantage of building a Power BI dashboard is that the data and calculations are visible. Instead of accepting a recommendation without explanation, users can see how different variables such as price, RAM, operating system, and brand contribute to the overall analysis.

Transparent reporting helps people better understand how conclusions are reached and builds trust in data-driven decisions.

Accountability#

Businesses benefit greatly from data analytics, but they are also responsible for ensuring their AI systems are fair, accurate, and respectful of consumer privacy. Ethical data practices are not only about following regulations; they are also about building trust with customers.

Working on this project showed me that data analytics is about much more than creating charts and dashboards. Every dataset represents real people and real decisions. As AI becomes more involved in our daily lives, data analysts have an important role in ensuring that insights are accurate, understandable, and ethically responsible.

This project helped me strengthen my Power BI skills while reinforcing an important lesson: great analytics is not only about finding patterns in data. It is also about using those insights responsibly. As I continue developing my skills in data analytics and artificial intelligence, I hope to build solutions that combine technical knowledge with ethical decision-making.

Power BI Dashboard#

To put these ethical concepts into practice, I developed a Power BI dashboard using a smartphone dataset. The dashboard explores purchasing trends, pricing, operating systems, and hardware specifications to demonstrate how data analytics can support business decision-making. At the same time, it highlights why ethical considerations such as privacy, bias, transparency, and accountability are essential when AI systems analyze consumer data.

The dashboard includes visualizations of smartphone brand popularity, average prices, price trends over time, operating system market share, RAM comparisons, and summary metrics. Together, these visuals demonstrate how raw data can be transformed into meaningful insights that support informed and responsible decisions.

Creating this project helped me better understand how data analytics and artificial intelligence work together to solve real-world problems. While dashboards and visualizations provide valuable business insights, they also remind us that every dataset represents real people and real decisions.

As AI continues to evolve, ethical principles such as privacy, fairness, transparency, and accountability will remain essential. Developing technical skills is important, but using those skills responsibly is what helps build trustworthy and effective AI systems.
